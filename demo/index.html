scrollfilm

A film you scrub with the scroll

Every frame below is a still from a rendered film. Scroll and the film plays under your finger, forwards or back, at any speed, on a phone as well as a computer.

Only the frames around the playhead are decoded, by the device's hardware decoder, and go straight to the GPU.

The film follows your scroll on a spring. It never runs ahead of what is decoded, so it never jumps.

Flick hard: the film travels through keyframes, which decode on their own, in either direction.

Stop anywhere and it settles on the exact frame.

Use it

npx scrollfilm encode film.mp4 public/film --width 1280
import { Film } from 'scrollfilm';

const film = new Film(document.querySelector('canvas'));
film.load('/film/manifest.json');
film.follow(document.querySelector('.track'));
